# Participant User Guide

For Micheal Ray Berry (the participant). The app helps you complete and document
each project day correctly and on time. It does **not** decide compliance — the
Accountability Partner (AP) does — and deadlines run on the server whether or
not the app is open.

## Signing in

Use your Google account or email/password. Biometric re-entry unlocks the app
after the first sign-in. Your permissions come from the server; you cannot grant
yourself AP powers.

## Today

The home screen shows the full date, your **project day number**, the applicable
deadline, and a **live countdown** synced to server time. Each requirement is a
card with its state and a single next action. The status strip shows whether
you're connected, working offline, or have an upload pending.

Requirement states move: Not started → In progress → Submitted → Under review →
Verified. If the AP finds a problem you'll see **Deficient** with instructions.

## Recording the daily video

1. **Preflight**: confirm the date, permissions, storage, and orientation.
2. **Guided recording**: follow the on-screen sequence and teleprompter (name,
   date, day, weight, the position steps, closing statement). Each step has a
   minimum hold time.
3. **Review**: play it back and answer the checklist. You may **accept** or
   **re-record**. You cannot trim, filter, splice, or edit the video — that's by
   design, to keep the evidence trustworthy.
4. **Upload**: the app uploads reliably in the background and resumes if the
   connection drops or the app restarts. A video is only "submitted" once the
   **server returns a receipt** — a local recording is not a submission.

## Weight & tracking

Enter your documented weight (one decimal). Unusual values ask for confirmation;
nothing is changed silently. If you need to fix a value later, submit a
**correction** with a reason — the original is preserved and the AP reviews it.

## External links

For website/X requirements, paste the public URL. The AP confirms it's
accessible at the required visibility.

## Notices, deficiencies, violations

- **Deficiency**: shows exactly what's missing and the correction deadline.
  Respond with supplemental or replacement evidence, or a factual explanation.
- **Violation**: you can **acknowledge** it. You cannot dismiss or edit it. If
  you believe there's an **objective factual error**, request a factual review —
  the AP decides.

## Timeliness

The server's received time determines whether a submission is on time. Setting
your phone's clock does nothing to the official deadline. Submitting a technical-
failure report preserves evidence for the AP but does not by itself excuse a
missed requirement.

## Your records

History shows every day with its status. You can export your records (PDF/CSV/
JSON) from the Project screen.

## Web controls (NextDNS)

The AP administers DNS filtering on your Pixel (Private DNS profile RAY-PIXEL)
and the home network (HOME-ROUTER). In the Web Controls portal you can see every
control's status, **request temporary access** (service, duration, reason),
follow your requests, view integrity status and incidents, and read the full
audit history, including when the AP viewed DNS activity and in which mode.

Rule: accountability controls must remain enabled and configured as the AP
directs. Do not disable, replace, circumvent or materially alter one without
prior AP approval, except when reasonably necessary for safety, emergency
access or essential device recovery. You keep physical control of your phone.
If you must interrupt monitoring for one of those reasons, the interruption is
recorded, and you explain it at AP review. Loss of monitoring does not suspend
the underlying requirement.
